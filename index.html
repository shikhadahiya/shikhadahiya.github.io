The case for PM

Three lenses on my career

Engineering depth — seen through a product eye.

The Systems Thinker
At Bloomberg, I led a full Linux OMS migration with zero downtime for 75+ traders, designed async stress testing infrastructure, and built capacity monitoring that changed how the team planned for scale.
  • Zero-downtime migration of trading-critical infrastructure
  • Async stress test platform — shifted ops from reactive to proactive
  • Capacity monitoring vision: from idea to organisational adoption
The Platform Builder
At Microsoft, I worked across Cosmos DB, Azure, and Bing Search — spanning globally distributed data, cloud infrastructure, and search relevance at enormous scale.
  • Cosmos DB: consistency, partitioning, and distributed trade-offs
  • Bing Search: relevance models, user signals, product iteration
  • Azure infrastructure: cross-team delivery and platform strategy
The Founder
Credimate is my most PM-native work — discovering a real gap in India's informal lending market, building UX prototypes, and pitching investors. Full ownership from insight to roadmap.
  • User discovery: informal credit in India's trust networks
  • Built UX prototype and customer journey maps from scratch
  • Investor pitch preparation and business model design
